Biography

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Marketing at the Mike Ilitch School of Business, Wayne State University. My research examines how misleading marketing communications shape consumer perceptions and decisions.

My dissertation focuses on claim–disclaimer inconsistency and how conflicting product information can increase perceived deception and undermine consumer responses. A related stream examines inflated reference pricing and how consumers respond to potentially misleading price information. Across these projects, I study when persuasive tactics backfire and how clearer marketing communication can benefit consumers, firms, and the marketplace.
